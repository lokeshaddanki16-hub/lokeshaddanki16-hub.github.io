SuperstarGambit Uploader

SuperstarGambit Uploader is a private, single-user desktop tool used by Lokesh Addanki to manage his own YouTube channel, SuperstarGambit (@superstargambit), which publishes original Indian-cinema commentary.

What it does

  • Uploads the channel owner's original Shorts (1–2 per day) and one long-form video per week using the YouTube Data API.
  • Sets titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and scheduled publish times for those videos.
  • Reads the owner's own channel statistics (YouTube Analytics API, read-only) to produce a private weekly report.

It is run by the owner's personal AI assistant, accesses only the owner's own channel after Google sign-in and consent, and does not share data with anyone else. It is not available to other users.

This tool uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
